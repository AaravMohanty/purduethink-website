Featured engagements.

PurdueTHINK · Internal
01

ThinkedIN

Our alumni network lived in group chats and personal contacts, and every client pitch was rebuilt from scratch. THINK Internal scoped and built ThinkedIN to fix both.

  • A searchable directory that keeps alumni reachable after graduation
  • A shared library of pitch and project material
  • Scoped, built and shipped by members, start to finish

02

Getting students into Mackey on a weeknight

Attendance was running well below what the arena holds. The team went after why students stay home, rather than why they ought to turn up.

  • Surveyed students on what actually keeps them away
  • Benchmarked how peer Big Ten programs fill seats
  • Designed game-day campaigns around what came back

Purdue Dining & Culinary
03

Reading the crowd at Wiley

Dining halls were crowding at peak hours as enrollment grew, and the complaints were about queues rather than food.

  • Worked through Occuspace occupancy data hall by hall
  • Paired the numbers with student feedback on wait times
  • Mapped where queues form and what could realistically shift
Data & operations analysis
Purdue RHA
04

Turning campus survey data into prevention strategy

The Residence Hall Association asked for help understanding gender violence data on campus, and for a plan to act on what it showed.

  • Ran data collection and analysis as one workstream
  • Turned the findings into a strategy and implementation plan
  • Focused on campus events that support prevention work
