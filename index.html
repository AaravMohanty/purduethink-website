Who we are

Student-run consulting at Purdue since 2016.

PurdueTHINK is Purdue’s leading and oldest student-run consultancy. For over a decade, we’ve partnered with organizations across campus to solve their hardest problems, giving our members real, hands-on project experience while helping build a better Purdue.

We also invest heavily in our members beyond client work. Internally, that means casework and mentorship from some of the most accomplished consultants on campus. Externally, it means an industry network that opens doors most students don’t see until years into their career. And underneath all of it is a community that actually shows up for each other, in casework, on projects, and everywhere in between.

Adaptable
Projects change shape partway through, and members adjust with them.
Collaborative
Teams are built from different majors on purpose, because most problems need more than one background.
Impact driven
Most of the work is for Purdue itself, so members can point at what they built and see it still running.
