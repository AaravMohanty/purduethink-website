The Interview

What makes consulting interviews different?

Interviews for consulting firms usually consist of a behavioral interview and a case interview. A case interview is when you are given a business problem and asked to solve it. Although it may sound simple, case interviews often intimidate people, and they can be intense and sometimes unpredictable. A few things that tend to help are below.

The interview

Forty minutes, in two parts.

This is mostly us getting to know you. You might be asked about a time you improved a team, or talked people into a decision they were not sold on at first. We are also curious what brought you here.

There may be brain teasers and case-style questions. If you have questions about THINK, this is a good time to ask them.

Part one 20 MIN
Behavioral

Much like a regular job interview. Most people find it helps to think through a few examples beforehand and say them out loud once or twice.

Part two 20 MIN
Case scenario

A business problem, worked through out loud. There is rarely one right answer, and we are more interested in how you structure it than where you land. Questions and notes are both fine.

Ace the Case

How to ace case interviews.

01
Always ask questions
The interviewer will give you the most important information about the case, but they will also give you the chance to get more by asking.
02
Structure your answer
There is usually more than one approach to a case, but you need to show your way of thinking. Gather your thoughts, jot down notes, and outline the logical structure of your solution.
03
Learn your case types
There is an almost infinite number of cases you could be given, but there are recognizable case “types” that become clear once you practice.
04
Practice your cases
Reading through cases, practicing with a friend, doing case competitions. These are just some of the ways to get comfortable with case interviews.
05
Join a consulting club on campus.
